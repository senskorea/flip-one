A forward slash with purpose.

The wordmark is direct, technological and optimistic. The slash connects two ideas: Korea and the world, founder and crew, uncertainty and direction. Try it below.

Clear space

Keep at least the height of the letter I around every side of the wordmark. Nothing enters that zone.

Approved colours

Flip Blue on light backgrounds. Pure white on Flip Blue and Deep Navy. Ink for print and monochrome.

Never alter

Do not stretch, outline, shadow, rotate or replace the slash. The slash stays a plain Arial solidus at 90% size.
