# Copy clarity pass

## Main findings

The site mixed venture studio, platform and advisory language. Maritime metaphors hid the actual work. Several sections repeated people, markets and funding without explaining the next action.

## Editorial direction applied

Lead with a Korea-based venture studio that builds with founders for equity. Keep growth services distinct. Describe People as a small curated directory, not a large network. Use plain labels for resources, events and enquiry actions. Preserve funding limitations and distinguish form previews from submissions.

## Draft and final review

Draft: “We help founders navigate their next stage with practical support.”

What makes this sound AI generated? The actor is clear but the work is not. “Navigate”, “next stage” and “practical support” could describe almost any consultancy.

Final: “We work alongside founders to test ideas and build the first product. We take equity in the ventures we co-build, with roles and terms agreed upfront.”

The homepage method is now Validate, Build, Grow. Its numbered layout replaces the illustrated service cards. Shared English and Korean labels were revised together, and matching Korean HTML fallback text was updated.

## Boundaries and remaining checks

This pass covers shared page copy, page headings, navigation destinations, form labels and the homepage method. Individual profile claims and long resource articles have not been rewritten or fact-checked. Event dates, availability and registration destinations still need owner confirmation. No funding eligibility claims were expanded. English URLs, canonical and social metadata remain a separate implementation task. Korean editorial review by a native speaker is recommended before launch.
