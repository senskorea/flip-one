# Flip One scroll scene prompt guide

## Purpose

These prompts reinterpret the original maritime story in the visual language of the current Flip One website. They are intended as experimental keyframes for a future scroll sequence, not locked production artwork.

## Continuity locks

| Element | Locked direction |
|---|---|
| Visual style | Minimal editorial 3D illustration, precise geometric forms, premium architectural visualisation, restrained faceting, translucent liquid glass and polished matte surfaces. Deep Navy `#09162F`, Flip Blue `#1647D8`, Electric Blue `#2457F5`, Warm Paper `#F7F7F3`, Signal Coral `#FF6B5E` and white. Use strong value contrast, rim light and clean silhouettes so subjects remain readable at thumbnail scale. Spacious, sleek and modern. |
| Founder | Mina, a Korean woman in her early thirties with a short asymmetric black bob and angular coral glasses. Intelligent, determined and approachable. She wears a structured warm ivory jacket, bright white top, tailored deep teal trousers, white trainers and a minimal silver smartwatch. A narrow coral collar detail identifies her at wide distances. Render her as a clean stylised geometric 3D character, never photorealistic. Keep her clothing distinct from both the navy ocean and coral hull. |
| Crew | Three visually secondary geometric characters: a Korean market and funding navigator with a slim tablet, an international partnerships specialist holding a translucent map, and a founder wellbeing coach carrying no prop. Give each a different high contrast block of warm white, Flip Blue or pale silver clothing with small coral details. Avoid dark navy outfits against night backgrounds. No uniforms or nautical costumes. |
| Hero boat | One sleek abstract sailboat with a Signal Coral hull, deep navy keel and trim, sharp warm white main sail, translucent liquid glass secondary sail and electric blue edge light. The coral hull must remain clearly separated from blue water in every scene. Elegant, geometric and recognisable at small scale. No antique ship, pirate styling or detailed rigging. |
| Format | Landscape 16:9. Preserve character, boat, palette and material continuity. Leave generous negative space for website copy. No text, typography, logos, watermark or decorative border. Each prompt remains under 89 words. |

## Suggested generation order

1. Generate Scene 01 until Mina and the hero boat are stable.
2. Reuse Scene 01 as the visual reference for Scenes 02 and 03.
3. Lock the crew from Scene 03 before generating Scene 04.
4. Generate Scene 05 from Scene 04 to preserve boat and lighting continuity.
5. Generate Scene 06 separately as a cleaner editorial closing frame.

For scroll implementation, treat these as anchor keyframes. Intermediate motion should be created through camera movement, object transforms, route line animation and crossfades rather than generating a large number of unrelated images.
